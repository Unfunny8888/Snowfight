Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Snowfight ("the app") does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third‑party tracking of any kind.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for or gather your name, email, contacts, location, photos, identifiers, or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your high score, best level, and sound/music preferences are saved locally on your device using the operating system's standard settings storage. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Nearby multiplayer

The "Nearby" mode lets two devices play together using Apple's local‑network technology (MultipeerConnectivity) over Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth. During a match, only real‑time gameplay information (player and snowball positions, scores) is exchanged directly between the two devices on your local network. Nothing is sent to any server, nothing is recorded, and no personal information is included. When the match ends, no data is retained.

Third parties

The app contains no third‑party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics services. No data is shared with anyone.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. Because we collect no personal information, none is ever knowingly collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
