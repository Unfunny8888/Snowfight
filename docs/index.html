How to play

  • Move your kids out of the ice dome to fight — kids inside are safe but can't throw.
  • Tap the enemy side to throw a snowball there (with aim assist), or drag from a kid to aim manually.
  • Tap your own side to move the selected kid.
  • Use snow forts for cover, grab power-ups, and smash the other team's dome to flush them out.

Game modes

  • Solo — endless levels against the computer.
  • 2 Players · 1 Device — split-screen versus, best of five rounds.
  • Nearby — two devices on the same Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth. Open Nearby on both and they find each other automatically.

Frequently asked

Nearby multiplayer isn't finding the other device.

Make sure both devices are on the same Wi‑Fi network (or have Bluetooth on), that both have opened Nearby, and that you allowed the local‑network permission prompt the first time. Toggling Airplane Mode off/on or reopening the game usually helps.

Does the game collect my data?

No. Snowfight collects no personal data, has no ads, no accounts, and no in‑app purchases. Your high score and settings stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

Is there a cost?

Snowfight is free to play with no purchases.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 16 or later. The game plays in landscape.
