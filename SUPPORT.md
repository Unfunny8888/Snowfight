# Snowfight — Support

Thanks for playing **Snowfight**, the classic snowball battle!

## Contact

Questions, bugs, or feedback? Email **winterbattle@delhiinmongol.com** and we'll get back to you.

## How to play

- **Move your kids out of the ice dome** to fight — kids inside are safe but can't throw.
- **Tap the enemy side** to throw there (with aim assist), or **drag from a kid** to aim manually.
- **Tap your own side** to move the selected kid.
- Use snow forts for cover, grab power-ups, and smash the enemy dome to flush them out.

## Modes

- **Solo** — endless levels vs. the computer.
- **2 Players · 1 Device** — split-screen versus, best of five rounds.
- **Nearby** — two devices on the same Wi‑Fi/Bluetooth; open *Nearby* on both and they connect automatically.

## FAQ

**Nearby isn't connecting?** Put both devices on the same Wi‑Fi (or enable Bluetooth), open *Nearby* on both, and allow the local‑network prompt. Reopening the app usually helps.

**Do you collect my data?** No — no data collection, no ads, no accounts, no purchases. See the [Privacy Policy](https://unfunny8888.github.io/snowfight/privacy.html).

**Supported devices?** iPhone and iPad on iOS 16 or later, landscape.
